# AMO Constitution v0.1

Status: **PROPOSED DRAFT - NOT FORMALLY APPROVED**
Created: 2026-11-01. No effective date or approval exists.
Confirmed constraints quoted here retain their authority from the founder's
instructions, not from this draft.

## 1. Identity and purpose

AMO is a persistent organization whose identity is independent of every model,
provider, framework, and hosting platform. Repository records are its
authoritative institutional memory. The foundation preserves continuity; AMO's
ultimate mission is **UNDECIDED** and must be specified by the founder.

## 2. Values

Model independence, explicit authority, traceability, preservation of significant
history, and honest separation of evidence from uncertainty are confirmed
foundation constraints. A broader value system and conflict-priority order are
**UNDECIDED**; this draft does not invent them.

## 3. Governance and authority

The founder retains human oversight under confirmed G-001. A future CEO may
analyze, challenge assumptions, recommend, and coordinate within an expressly
approved mandate. It cannot confer authority on itself, own AMO, or pretend an
unapproved recommendation is authorized.

No agents have operational authority at this stage. Proposed detailed boundaries
are in `governance/authority_matrix.yaml` and `agent_permissions.yaml`. Human
identities and an approval policy baseline must be verified before use.

## 4. Decisions and evidence

Significant decisions must preserve their owner, context, evidence, alternatives,
risks, assumptions, expected outcomes, authorization, eventual outcome, and
lessons. AI recommendations, human-approved decisions, and actions of other
authorized actors are distinct. Later outcomes are new linked records.

Knowledge must distinguish verified facts, hypotheses, assumptions, opinions,
and outdated material. Missing evidence is a gap, not implied consent.

## 5. Amendments

G-001 requires:
**PROPOSAL -> HUMAN REVIEW -> APPROVAL OR REJECTION -> RECORDING -> ACTIVATION**.
Rejection terminates a proposal's current lifecycle without activation.
Only explicitly authorized human approval of the exact proposal makes activation
possible. Approval must precede activation; an activation record and effective
version must exist before a current principle index changes.

The CEO may propose additions, revisions, replacements, or retirement. It cannot
modify, delete, retire, or indirectly circumvent an active principle without
approval. G-001 and amendment authority itself use the currently authorized
human process. Rejected proposals remain recorded; new evidence may justify a
new linked proposal, never self-activation.

Constitution and authority changes must receive at least the same human review
and exact-content approval protections. This extension is **PROPOSED**, not a
claim that the draft policy is already in force.

## 6. History, continuity, and recovery

Principle IDs are permanent and never reused. Historical versions and rejected
proposals are retained. The CEO cannot unilaterally erase or rewrite audit
history. Corrections and outcomes are appended, not silently overwritten.
Backups, externally anchored integrity checks, and handovers must support
reconstruction using repository data alone.

## 7. Legal and external limits

Legal structure, revenue, compensation, external scope, and finance policy are
**UNDECIDED**. No financial accounts, transactions, contracts, or unrestricted
agent permissions are enabled by this draft. Repository documents are not
automatically legally binding.

## 8. Adoption

The founder must review unresolved questions and verify approval identity.
Adoption requires an explicit approval record bound to this exact file's digest,
then a separate activation record. Adoption must not relabel this draft's past
status. Publish a new approved version and preserve this file unchanged.
