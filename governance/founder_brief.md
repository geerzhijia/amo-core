# Founding instruction record

Status: **CONFIRMED source transcription**, recorded 2026-11-01.
Source: human founder's AMO Core initialization request supplied to the repository
initializer. This is a faithful scoped summary, not a signed original transcript
or independent identity attestation.

The founder requested:

- AMO means Autonomous Mission Organization and must persist across replacement
  of all models, frameworks, infrastructure, and storage.
- The repository is authoritative; vendor memory is not.
- Establish documentation, schemas, governance, and validation, not a runtime.
- Record G-001 as a confirmed initial design decision: the CEO may propose
  principle changes but only explicitly authorized humans may approve them.
  Proposal, human review, approval/rejection, recording, and activation are
  distinct stages. Rejections and historical versions must remain available.
  Changing amendment authority uses the current human-governed process.
- The constitution must remain a draft until formally approved.
- Do not invent the mission, legal structure, revenue model, compensation policy,
  or external operating scope; these remain UNDECIDED.
- Preserve rationale, evidence, authorization, outcomes, and institutional history.
- Do not enable autonomous execution, connect finances, transact, or give AI
  unrestricted access. Do not equate Git with immutable storage or documents
  with legal establishment.

DEC-0001 records these instructions, not an initializer-generated approval.
Authenticated approval identities, cryptographic signatures, and a policy
baseline still require founder review. No GitHub owner or commit author
automatically becomes an enrolled approver.
