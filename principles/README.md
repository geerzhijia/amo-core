# Principles

`registry.json` is the current index. Each entry has a permanent ID, title,
description, rationale, scope, priority, status, creation date, semantic version,
source/authorization, related IDs, and a version document.

G-001 v1.0.0 is the only initial active principle. Its authority comes from the
confirmed founder instruction, not approval of the draft constitution.
Its numeric priority is **UNDECIDED**; its approval boundary is mandatory.

`history/principle_evolution/G-001-v1.0.0.md` is the immutable initial version.
The friendly top-level document points to it; later versions get new files.
Retired IDs remain in the index forever and may not be reassigned. Proposed
principles live in amendment records until activation.

Changing the current registry is not authorization. Any version/status/document
change beyond the bootstrap G-001 needs a matching activated amendment.
Descriptions, rationale, and other metadata also belong to the approved revision.
Do not disguise substantive changes as editorial updates. Whole policy and
authorization review remains a human responsibility.
