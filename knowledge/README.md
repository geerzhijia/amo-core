# Organizational knowledge

Status: **PROPOSED recording convention**. No research claims are seeded.

For each real knowledge item record a permanent ID, title, author, observed and
recorded dates, claim, classification, source/evidence references, confidence,
review date, applicability, and links to decisions or principles.

Use explicit classifications: `verified_fact` (describe verification),
`hypothesis` (testable but unverified), `assumption` (used provisionally),
`opinion` (attributed judgment), or `outdated` (preserved with superseding link).
Distinguish a source's assertion from an independently verified fact.

Append dated corrections and lessons; retain prior claims and explain why they
changed. External URLs are references, not durable evidence by themselves.
Record retrieval dates and retain lawful, non-sensitive exportable evidence
where necessary. Sensitive evidence belongs in controlled storage, not a public
repository; flag missing evidence during handover.

Research/reference and lessons directories are deferred until actual items exist.
